## Why him

There are three Russian-language records about this war on my shelf, and this is the one made by someone whose catalogue was already pointed the right way before it cost anything to point it. "Mercedes S666" is from 2010 and it is about a rich man killing people with a car and walking away. He held a Ukrainian flag over his head in Lviv in 2014 and went home to cancelled concerts and police attention. None of that was a reaction to February 2022 — February 2022 just made everyone else catch up.

That is what makes him listenable to me in a way most of his peers aren't. He didn't discover a position when the position became expensive; he had held one for fifteen years, and the "foreign agent" tag is basically the receipt. [[Выход в город]] came out two months before the invasion and reads completely differently now. [[Open Air]] was written after, from the outside, for rooms full of people who also can't go back.

Being right early didn't save anyone. It does mean the songs still work.
