## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Released | 2014 |
| Album | Матрёшка |
| Length | 3:44 |

*Воины света* came out on *Матрёшка* on 1 March 2014 and was already past the band by then: a fan-cut video went up the day before the official one and ran away with millions of views, and the song became the unofficial anthem of the Maidan.

Lyapis Trubetskoy broke up later that year and Mikhalok kept playing it with Brutto. In 2022 the band re-recorded it in Ukrainian, in a translation by Serhiy Zhadan — which is the version that matters now.
