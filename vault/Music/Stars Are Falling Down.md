*Stars Are Falling Down* came out on 20 April 2023, made with ATLVNTA and Kobzaruk and put together through Music Export Ukraine. Brunettes Shoot Blondes have been going since 2010, out of Kryvyi Rih, and are better known abroad than most Ukrainian indie bands — which is exactly the reach this song was written to use.

The band were direct about the purpose: draw the attention of listeners in other countries, and remind them the Russian invasion is still going. That is a different job from writing for people already inside it — and it is the same job [[Bulletproof|BLIND8]] set themselves.
