*Полароід* is on *Переклад*, out on 21 June 2018 — the second solo album Stepan Burban made under the name Кашляючий Ед, a year before he retired it for [[Літній час|Паліндром]]. Eleven tracks, thirty-eight minutes.

He had been at this a while by then: [[Найгірша пісня про любов|Глава 94]], the Lviv group he started in 2010 with [[Як|Гоня]], was eight years old and still running. The two names are one person, which is worth knowing before the discography confuses you.
