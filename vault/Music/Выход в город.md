*Выход в город* came out in halves — ten tracks on 19 November 2021, the full twenty on 17 December. It is written as one thing rather than collected as twenty: a narrator working his way out of whatever is closing in on him, which is where the title comes from.

It arrived two months before the full-scale invasion, and the years after it read differently than the album does. Ivan Alekseyev left Russia in 2022 and has played anti-war and fundraising shows since; that November, Russia's Justice Ministry put him on its register of "foreign agents".
