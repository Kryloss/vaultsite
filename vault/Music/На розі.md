*На розі* arrived on 3 October 2025, ahead of *У тіні параної* — the debut album that followed on 14 November, on kontrabass. Three old friends made it: Oleksandr Zaika sings, Bohdan Makeienko plays guitar, Valerii Nizhynets drums.

The record is about their own heads more than anything outside them — the band has described it as working their difficulties out into songs — and the title comes from a line in one of them.
