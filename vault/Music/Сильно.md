*Сильно* is on *Схожі*, out on 18 September 2020 — six numbered songs plus two bonus tracks, and her first album. Схожа works in a mix of indie pop, R&B and Ukrainian folk, which is a combination that usually collapses into one of the three and here does not.

The feature is Марний, the Kyiv indie-rock duo of Andriy Dorosh and Yakiv Marnyi, formed in 2018. That collaboration outlasted the guest spot: two of the five EPs they released over the following three years were made with her.
