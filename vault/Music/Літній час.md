*Літній час* came out on 15 July 2022, on its own. It is the same man who made [[Полароід]] four years earlier as Кашляючий Ед — Паліндром is the name he took in 2019, and the point of taking it was that the music was going somewhere the old name did not fit.

[[Найгірша пісня про любов|Глава 94]], the Lviv group he and [[Як|Гоня]] founded in 2010, was still nominally going when this came out; it closed for good the following July.
