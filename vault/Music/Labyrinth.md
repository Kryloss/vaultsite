*Labyrinth* came out on 12 September 2024, BLIND8 with Sharon den Adel and Within Temptation. It is the thing [[Bulletproof]] was aiming at: a Ukrainian band getting heard by an audience that would otherwise never have gone looking, and then going out on the BLEED OUT tour with them.

Two of the band were serving while this happened, which is the detail that makes the collaboration read as something other than a career step.
