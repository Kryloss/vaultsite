*Найгірша пісня про любов* is on *Вафлі*, eight tracks credited to the group rather than to any one of them. Глава 94 was founded in Lviv in 2010 by [[Полароід|Stepan Burban]] and [[Як|Ihor Honia]], and took its name from Psalm 94 — the one that opens by calling everyone to sing.

[[Amplitude|Sonnium]] joined in January 2021, a few months before this came out, which makes *Вафлі* one of the few records with all three of them on it. The group closed for good on 17 July 2023 and the three went their own ways, which is why they turn up separately elsewhere on this page.
