*Ти* came out on 19 April 2024. Ницо Потворно is Serhiy Husak, who is an ENT doctor as well as a musician — his debut mini-album *Револьвер 13* landed in January 2019 and *Реанімація* in 2020 is what got him noticed.

When mobilisation came he had options, including the Cultural Landing Force, and chose to serve as an ENT doctor with the National Guard. The songs kept coming anyway; so did the podcast.
