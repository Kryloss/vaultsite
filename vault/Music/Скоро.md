*Скоро* came out on 21 March 2022, a month into the full-scale invasion, and joined *Незвана вечеря* when the EP followed on 15 April. Krechet works anonymously — the mask is not a stage prop he sometimes wears, it is the entire public face — and raps in Ukrainian in a mumble register that the language does not have much of.

He started the double album *Українофікація* the same day the EP came out, and had it finished by July.
