*Щастя під час* is on *Карти на стіл*, out on 9 December 2022 and credited to Міша Правильний and ValeryBlahBlah jointly rather than as a guest spot. The album is put together with skits between the songs — *Боротьба*, *Дельфіни* — so it is meant to be heard in order.

The title is the whole idea: happiness *during*, with the sentence left unfinished because everyone listening in December 2022 could finish it themselves.
