*Летимо* is on *Неймовірна простота*, out on 20 June 2019, with the Kyiv group Тулим on the feature and their Kulia producing. Zombo came out of Kickit, the Lviv collective, and his roots are in the 2000s scene — which is audible, because the beat here is deliberately old-school rather than anything from 2019.
