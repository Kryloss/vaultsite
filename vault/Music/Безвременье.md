*Безвременье* opens *Длится февраль*, the album Vladi of Каста put out in December 2022. Eight tracks, and the subject is not the war at a distance but the people around him and what each of them decided to believe about it. This one is the prologue and the most general of them; the rest go case by case.

Meduza called it the most uncompromising album about the war by a Russian musician, on the grounds that he almost never reaches for a metaphor. It belongs on a short shelf with [[Это пройдёт|Порнофильмы]] and [[Выход в город|Noize MC]] — and it is the one of the three made by someone who stayed.
