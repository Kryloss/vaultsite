*РЕБЕЛІЯ [1991]* premiered as a stage production on 21 August 2025 and went out to everyone else three days later, on Independence Day. It runs from the Twentieth Congress to the vote — samvydav, the sixtiers, Stus and Chornovil, Chornobyl, Rukh — and it is the chronological sequel to МУР's earlier *Ти [Романтика]*, which covered the Executed Renaissance.

The words are Oleksandr Khomenko's and the music Viktor Tkachenko's; almost every track is sung by a guest, which over 24 of them turns into a fair map of who is making Ukrainian music right now — MONATIK, Artem Pivovarov, Jerry Heil, Klavdia Petrivna, NAZVA, OTOY, Kazhanna, and journalists reading rather than singing.

The same ground in book form is [[Illustrated History of Ukraine's Independence]], and the April 1986 stretch of it is what [[Chernobyl]] dramatises from the other side.
