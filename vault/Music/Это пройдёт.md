*Это пройдёт* is the title track of the record Pornofilmy released on 29 January 2020. The band is from Dubna and has spent its whole career as one of the few Russian punk bands willing to name what it is angry about, which is what makes a title like this one read as something other than comfort.

What came after is the part that dates it. The band moved to Tbilisi in February 2022, and in September 2023 Vladimir Kotlyarov was added to Russia's register of "foreign agents" — the same list that has [[Выход в город|Noize MC]] on it.
