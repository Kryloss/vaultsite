*Як?* is on *Ні в кут, ні в двері*, out on 16 September 2022, with Jointjay on the feature. Ihor Honia is the other founder of [[Найгірша пісня про любов|Глава 94]] — he and [[Полароід|Stepan Burban]] started it in Lviv in 2010, and it finally closed in July 2023.

This EP belongs to the stretch where both of them were mostly working under their own names, which is what a group winding down usually looks like from the outside.
