*CHORT* sits on *CVIT*, the December 2020 EP that OTOY treats as his real debut. The title is not a general-purpose devil: paired with the then-interior-minister's surname it had become one of the chants of the protests against police violence, and the track is about that.

Viacheslav Drofa was born in Lviv in 1998 and studied software development before any of this. He turns up again singing on [[РЕБЕЛІЯ 1991|МУР's РЕБЕЛІЯ]], and in 2023 he rapped Shevchenko at Eurovision, which tells you roughly where the range sits.
