*Твій на 100%* was the second single trailing the record that became *Таємний код. Рубікон*, after *Тримай мене* that summer. Andriy Khlyvnyuk has said he wrote it during a single flight, which is his explanation for the pace of it, and that it started from a story one of the band's listeners told him.

It is worth remembering what happened to that voice afterwards. Khlyvnyuk was in Kyiv when the full-scale invasion began, joined the territorial defence, and the phone video he filmed on Sofiiska Square went around the world.
